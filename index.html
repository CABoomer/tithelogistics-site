How it works

Your accounts, your rates

Connect the LTL carrier accounts you've already negotiated (XPO today, with more carriers on the way). Tithe Logistics never adds its own markup to freight. You can add one yourself if you choose.

Live rates at checkout

Cartons are packed onto your pallet sizes and rated with your carrier, so customers see real dock and liftgate delivery prices.

Book from the order

Pull a Shopify order into a shipment, book it with your carrier, and download the BOL and pallet labels. You can track it from the same place.

Pricing

Billed monthly through Shopify. You pay only for bookings confirmed with your carrier. Quotes and checkout rates are free.

Free

$0: 10 bookings total, to try it on real freight.

Starter

$29/month: 10 bookings included, then $5 each.

Growth

$89/month: 50 bookings included, then $4 each.

Pro

$149/month: 200 bookings included, then $3 each.

Tithe Logistics is software only. It is not a freight broker, freight forwarder or motor carrier. Shipments are contracted directly between you and your carrier under your own account.
