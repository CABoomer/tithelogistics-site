How it works

Your accounts, your rates

Connect the LTL carrier accounts you've already negotiated. Tithe Logistics never marks up freight.

Live rates at checkout

Customers see real freight quotes calculated from product weight, dimensions and carton/pallet configuration.

Book from the order

Create the shipment with your carrier straight from the Shopify order. You're billed only for confirmed bookings.

Tithe Logistics is software only. It is not a freight broker, freight forwarder or motor carrier. Shipments are contracted directly between you and your carrier under your own account.
